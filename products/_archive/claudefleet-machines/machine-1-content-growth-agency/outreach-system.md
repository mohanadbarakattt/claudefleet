# Outreach System — Landing Clients for the Content & Growth Offer

This is the base outreach system for this machine: a cold email sequence, a
warm-audience DM script, and objection handling. Use `agents/outreach-agent.md`
to personalize any of this per prospect — this file is the reusable base, not
a one-time draft.

## Where Apollo and Resend plug in

- **Apollo.io** is where you build the prospect list (filter by industry,
  follower/employee count proxies, location) and where you actually send the
  cold email sequence below — Apollo's sequencer handles the send schedule,
  reply detection, and stopping the sequence once someone replies. Load the
  4 emails below into an Apollo sequence as steps 1-4 with delays between them
  (suggested: 3-4 days between each — test and adjust based on your own reply
  data, don't treat 3-4 days as fixed).
- **Resend** is not for cold outreach — it's for transactional/deliverable
  email once someone is a client: sending the weekly content calendar, script
  batch, or delivery notification on schedule. Keep cold prospecting (Apollo)
  and client deliverable delivery (Resend) on separate systems so a
  deliverability issue on one never touches the other, and so client-facing
  email doesn't look like a mass sender.

## Cold email sequence (4 emails)

**Email 1 — Observation + offer**
Subject: `quick one about [Prospect]'s [platform] content`

> Hey [First Name],
>
> Noticed [Prospect]'s been posting on [platform] but not on a super
> consistent schedule the last few weeks — totally normal when you're running
> the business and the content at the same time.
>
> I run a small content system that hands [Prospect] 4 short-form
> videos/week — scripts, hooks, captions, and a posting schedule — planned
> around [their niche/audience] specifically, so you're not the bottleneck
> anymore.
>
> Worth a 10-minute look at what a week would actually look like for you?
>
> [Name]

**Email 2 — How it works (send if no reply)**
Subject: `here's how the content week actually works`

> Following up — figured a quick breakdown might be more useful than another
> "just checking in."
>
> Each week: I plan the themes around your niche and goals, write the full
> scripts + hooks + captions, and hand you a ready posting calendar. If you
> want, I can also turn the scripts into ready-to-generate video prompts so
> production is faster on your end.
>
> No pressure — just let me know if a short call makes sense this week.
>
> [Name]

**Email 3 — Objection-first (send if no reply)**
Subject: `re: content for [Prospect]`

> I'll guess what's on your mind — probably "does this actually sound like
> my brand, or generic AI content?"
>
> Fair question. Every script is written around your specific niche and
> audience, not a template, and you review everything before it goes out —
> nothing posts without your OK. Happy to send one sample script for your
> niche, no commitment, so you can judge the quality yourself.
>
> [Name]

**Email 4 — Break-up (send if no reply)**
Subject: `should I stop following up?`

> I'll take the silence as "not right now" — totally fine.
>
> If content ever becomes the thing eating your week, the offer's simple:
> 4 scripts + captions + a posting calendar, built around your niche, ready
> for you to post or hand to whoever's producing your videos.
>
> Door's open whenever. Good luck with everything in the meantime.
>
> [Name]

## Warm-audience DM script

Use when someone in your existing audience/DMs shows interest but hasn't
directly asked to buy.

> Hey! Yeah, I help [creators/brands] with exactly that — I plan and write
> short-form content (scripts, hooks, captions, posting schedule) so you're
> not stuck writing it all yourself every week.
>
> Quick questions so I can tell you if it's a fit: what platform(s) are you
> focused on, and is the goal more views/growth or more leads/sales from the
> content?
>
> [wait for answer, then:]
>
> Got it — that's exactly the kind of setup I build for. Want me to put
> together a sample week of content ideas for your niche so you can see how
> it'd actually look before we talk pricing?

Keep DMs short, conversational, and question-led — the goal of a warm DM is
to qualify and book a call, not to close in the thread.

## Objection handling

**"How is this different from just using ChatGPT myself?"**
> Totally could — the difference is the system: someone (me) planning your
> week around your actual niche and audience, writing it consistently, and
> handing you a ready calendar instead of you prompting and editing it
> yourself every week. You're buying back the hours, not the AI access.

**"How do I know the content will actually sound like my brand?"**
> Every script gets built around specifics you give me — your actual offer,
> audience, and tone — and nothing goes live until you've reviewed it. First
> week is the calibration round; if something's off-tone, I adjust before it
> ever gets close to your feed.

**"What if it doesn't get results?"**
> I can't promise views or sales — nobody honestly can. What I can promise
> is consistency: content actually goes out every week instead of stalling,
> which is what's usually actually costing creators/brands growth. If after
> a trial period it's clearly not working for your niche, we stop — no
> long-term lock-in required to start.
