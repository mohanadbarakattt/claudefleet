# Claude Money OS

An operating system for building a real, paying business around Claude — not a list of "AI side hustle" tips.

This is not a hype product. There are no income promises here, no screenshots of bank balances, no "I made $10k in a weekend." What you get instead: a repeatable system for picking a lane, packaging an offer, delivering it well with Claude doing the heavy lifting, finding your first clients, and scaling from "you doing the work" to "you running the operation."

Every number in this playbook that touches pricing or market rates is framed as a range you verify yourself on Upwork, Fiverr, or in your own outreach — markets move, and anyone selling you a fixed number is selling you a guess. Where you see "VERIFY:", that's a deliberate flag: go check current reality before you act on it.

## What you're buying

A sequence, not a library. Read it in order the first time. After that, it's a reference you return to at each stage of the business.

## Reading order

1. **01-pick-your-lane.md** — Five proven ways to make money with Claude as your workforce. A decision matrix scored on time, skill, capital, and speed-to-first-dollar. You'll leave with one lane picked, not five half-considered.
2. **02-the-offer.md** — How to turn a lane into a sellable offer. The offer equation, five fully-written example offers, and a three-tier pricing structure (floor/anchor/premium) so you're never guessing what to charge.
3. **03-delivery-engine.md** — The actual Claude workflows: prompt chains, a quality-control pass so you never ship raw AI output, a file system to stay organized, and turnaround targets that keep you fast without being sloppy.
4. **04-first-three-clients.md** — Three concrete methods to land your first clients, 10 ready-to-send outreach templates, and responses to the five objections you'll hear most.
5. **05-scale-with-agents.md** — The shift from doing the work to orchestrating it: Claude Code subagents as your first "employees," productizing your service, when to raise prices, and a weekly CEO-hour ritual.
6. **06-templates.md** — Copy-paste templates: proposal, scope of work, invoice line items, onboarding message, testimonial request, case study skeleton.
7. **07-90-day-plan.md** — A week-by-week plan from zero to a systemized, scaling operation, with daily non-negotiables and a KPI tracker.

## How to use this

- Don't skim. Do the exercises. The pick-one exercise in chapter 1 and the offer-writing in chapter 2 are where most people quit early — and where the entire business gets built.
- Keep a single working folder for your business (chapter 3 gives you the structure). Every template and prompt chain in this playbook assumes you have one.
- This assumes you have access to Claude or Claude Code. It does not assume any coding background — chapter 5 explains subagents in plain terms, no prior dev experience required.
- Revisit chapter 7's KPI tracker weekly. A business you don't measure is a hobby.

## What this is not

- Not a guarantee of income. Results depend on your effort, your market, and factors outside this document's control.
- Not legal, tax, or accounting advice. Set up your business entity and taxes with a licensed professional in your jurisdiction.
