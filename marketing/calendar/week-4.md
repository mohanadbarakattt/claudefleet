# ClaudeFleet — Content Calendar, Week 4 (Days 22–30)

**Arc: Cheat-code push.** Heaviest hype/cheat-code framing of the month. This
week carries the real-mechanics push on both machines, a dramatized Client
day showing a launch-kit deliverable, a bundle close (BOTH, $149), and closes
the month on Day 30 with an anti-pitch built entirely out of inspectable
evidence. Every CHEAT-CODE day is paired with a real screen-record per
content-system.md — the hype needs a receipt, not just a claim. No fake
balances/dashboards, no countdown urgency, no income guarantees anywhere in
this file.

**Faceless — see the block at the top of week-1.md before shooting.** Nobody
appears in frame on any day this week. Shot modes are tagged per day:
(a) screen-only + voiceover, (b) Ava, (c) text-on-screen with no voice.
**The Client** (Day 25) is now **voice-only and hands-only** — a hand on a
trackpad, a voice reacting off-screen — and stays labelled as a dramatization.
This is the hardest week to run faceless: it's the closing week, hype is at its
peak, and there's no presenter to absorb the ask. The compensation is that every
hype line this week lands on top of something a viewer could go and verify, and
Day 30 spends the whole slot on verifiable evidence and stated limitations
instead of on a person being sincere at the lens.
