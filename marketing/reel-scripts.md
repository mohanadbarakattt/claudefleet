# ClaudeStack — Instagram Reel Scripts (5)

Format per reel: HOOK (0–2s) → PLOT (2–20s) → PAYOFF (20–28s) → CTA (28–32s).
CTA keyword per reel is different so you can track which reel converts.
Voiceover = you or AI voice. On-screen text is written to be readable without sound.

---

## Reel 1 — "Your AI is unemployed" (sells: Power Pack) · keyword: **STACK**

**Hook (spoken):** "You have the smartest employee on earth… and it's sitting there unemployed."
**On-screen:** YOUR AI IS UNEMPLOYED.

**Plot beats:**
1. "Most people use Claude like Google — one question, one answer, close the tab."
2. "Meanwhile, some people gave it a job description. Actually — five job descriptions."
3. "An executor that builds. A reviewer that checks the work. A researcher, a marketer, a bug-hunter."
4. "That's not a chatbot anymore. That's a team that works while you sleep."

**Payoff:** "I packaged the exact 5 AI employees + 7 commands I use. Install time: 2 minutes."
**CTA (spoken + text):** "Comment **STACK** and I'll DM you the link."

## Reel 2 — "The $27 folder" (sells: Prompt Vault) · keyword: **VAULT**

**CTA:** "Comment **VAULT** for the link. First 50 comments get the discounted price."

## Reel 5 — "Stop scrolling AI tips" (sells: Vault or bundle) · keyword: **START**

**Hook:** "You've saved 400 AI tips. You've made $0. Here's why."
**On-screen:** 400 SAVED TIPS. $0 MADE.

**Plot beats:**
1. "Tips are entertainment. Systems make money."
2. "A tip is 'use ChatGPT for emails.' A system is: prompt → deliverable → price → who to send it to."
3. "That last part — the price and the buyer — is what every free tip leaves out."
4. "Because the person posting the tip doesn't want you to have the system. They want you to keep watching."

**Payoff:** "I sell the system. That's the whole business model. No course calls, no upsell funnels — a folder of systems."
**CTA:** "Comment **START** and I'll send you the one that fits where you are."
